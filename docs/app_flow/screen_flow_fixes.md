# Fixes for the design-tool screen flow (`docs/screen_flow.pdf`, 46 screens, 12 Nov)

The polished flow was drawn before the decisions of 12 Nov evening (`docs/change_log.md`). Its screens are pictures from the design tool, so they are fixed in the design tool, not here. Paste this list into the tool, export again to `docs/screen_flow.pdf`, and delete this file once every line is done.

Until then `docs/app_flow_v2.pdf` (generated by `build_flow.py`) is the version that matches the decisions.

## Everywhere

1. **Core staff = super admin.** Legend "Admin, core staff or super admin only" → "Admin or super admin only". Label "ADMIN, CORE STAFF" (Know My Constituency, pages 1 and 8) → "ADMIN · SUPER ADMIN".
2. **Area levels:** "AC → Village Panchayat → Part" → "AC → Village Panchayat / Municipal Council → Part" (Pernem town is a Municipal Council, not a village panchayat).
3. **Sample data:** VP names and voter names are samples; keep the footer "All data shown is sample data".

## Kutumb Connect (pages 1, 3, 4)

4. **Not made from house numbers.** Remove the note "Made automatically from House no. 14/2 …", the header "KUTUMB CONNECT · HOUSE 14/2", "Suggested by name …" and the Merge button. Header: the Kutumb name the surveyor typed (e.g. "Kutumb: Suresh Parab ✎").
5. Keep: Add member, Remove member, New kutumb, Move member. No family head anywhere.
6. **Where it opens:** from the Voter profile's Kutumb tab only. On the map and on page 3, "Kutumb Connect (tap a house)" → "Tap a house → its voters"; the walk list stays house by house.
7. "Message kutumb" and "Slips to kutumb" → one button "Share on WhatsApp (pick up to 5)".

## Messaging (pages 1, 3, 4)

8. **"Message many voters" → "Share on WhatsApp".** At most 5 voters: "8 voters selected" and "Group 1 / Group 2" go; a sixth voter cannot be ticked; a voter without a mobile is shown as skipped.
9. **No SMS here:** remove the "Send by: WhatsApp | SMS" choice and the SMS section. SMS stays only as the single-voter button on the Voter profile (with Call and WhatsApp).
10. Select many voters: the "Send message" action becomes "Share on WhatsApp", offered only when 5 or fewer voters are ticked.
11. Page 3 subtitle "send one message to many voters" → "share on WhatsApp with up to 5 voters".

## Survey and Filters (pages 3, 4)

12. **Survey screen:** check that it has, below "About the voter": Influencer Yes / No → searchable list (Sarpanch, Panch Member, Up Sarpanch, ZP Member, Ex. ZP Member, MLA, Ex. MLA, NGO, Social Worker, News Reporter, Social Media Influencer); Self help group Yes / No → group name; Ration card AAY / PHH / NPHH; colour tags Ours / Opposite / Doubtful (no white); no Important voter, no Two-wheeler, no Mandal / Group.
13. **Not in the SRS, take out** (or they become change requests the client will expect): Survey "Email", "Society / building", "Flat no.", "Demands / local issues", "Visit result" chips; Filters "Saved filters" and "Society". The SRS survey has mobiles, caste, profession, colour tag, residence check, date of birth, notes and the fields in item 12. (Visit result belongs to the walk list, not the survey form.)
14. Filters "Voted / Not voted / Slip printed / Marked dead" chips: fine to keep only if the client wants them; they are not in the SRS filter list (ward, booth, age, gender, colour tag, survey status, influencer, surname).

## Matching Engine (pages 1, 6)

15. "View results" opens a **Matching results** screen, not Search: counts and lists of matched, new, deleted and changed voters, "Apply this roll", and "Download report (Excel · PDF · CSV)". Deleted voters are "not in current roll", never erased; survey data stays.

## Admin (pages 1, 9)

16. Add **Village Panchayats** (ADMIN): list of panchayats and Pernem Municipal Council with their parts, "+ Add", "Edit"; a part belongs to exactly one.
17. Election & candidate: label "ADMIN · SUPER ADMIN" (both may add and edit); it has polling date and **polling time**.
