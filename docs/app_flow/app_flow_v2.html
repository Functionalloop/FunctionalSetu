Summary of changes

JANSETU · App flow and wireframes

Version 2 · 12 Nov 2026 · For: Naik Management Solutions Pvt Ltd · In reply to “App Flow Modifications & Additional Functional Requirements”

What this document is

  • The Android app flow with every requirement of your list placed in it: 51 screens (42 before). Page 2 is the map; pages 3–17 show each flow with its screens and what to tap.
  • The web portal has the same features with the same permissions (page 18). Pages 18–19 list who can do what and the points we need you to confirm.
  • Items marked Change request on page 19 are additions to the approved SRS (version 1.1). Their time and cost are sent separately; work on them starts after your approval.

NEW  9 new screens

  • Part note: One shared, synced note for each part. Opens from the walk list and the Survey tab.
  • Share on WhatsApp: The bulk option: up to 5 voters, through the phone's own WhatsApp. No WhatsApp API, no bulk SMS.
  • Report view: One screen for all eight reports: choose the level, set filters, download. Needs internet.
  • Matching results: Change request: newly added, deleted and changed voters after a comparison, with a report to download.
  • Know My Constituency: Change request: a new section in the More menu.
  • Geography: Panchayats, their parts and polling stations, with voter counts.
  • Demographics: Worked out from the roll and the survey data already in JANSETU.
  • Previous elections: Previous MP, AC and ZP election results.
  • Village Panchayats: Change request: the level between the constituency and the part.

RENAMED  5 renamed screens

  • Kutumb Connect (was “Family”): Made by the surveyor: a name and its members. No family head. Not created from house numbers (please confirm, see last page).
  • Information Updation (was “Voter history”): Renamed. Shows what changed (old and new value), who and when.
  • Eligible but Not Registered (was “New voter”): Renamed. “Not registered” and “New voter” are one category.
  • Eligible but Not Registered list (was “New voters list”): Renamed.
  • Matching Engine (was “Roll update”): Renamed and given its own item in the More menu.

CHANGED  16 changed screens

  • Voter survey: No Important voter field. Influencer: Yes / No, then one of the 11 entries. Also on this tab: mobile 2, residence check, date of birth.
  • Filters: Influencer filter replaces Important voters. New: Surname, Village Panchayat (was Ward).
  • Search: Surname search (change request): set in Filters, shown as a chip.
  • Voter profile: Tabs renamed: Family → Kutumb Connect, History → Information Updation. Ward → Village Panchayat. Call, WhatsApp and SMS stay.
  • Walk list: The part's shared note is on top. Houses still come from the roll's house numbers; a Kutumb is separate.
  • Voter slip: Polling time added; it comes from Election & candidate.
  • Select many voters: New action: Share on WhatsApp, offered when 5 or fewer voters are ticked.
  • Home: Shortcut renamed: was “New voter”.
  • More: New items: Know My Constituency, Matching Engine (its own item), Village Panchayats. Renamed: Eligible but Not Registered list.
  • Reports & charts: Change request: eight new reports, each at AC, Village Panchayat and Part level.
  • Print or export a list: CSV added next to Excel and PDF.
  • Election & candidate: Polling time added (printed on the slip). Admin and super admin can both add and edit.
  • Lists & labels: The influencer list with the Company's 11 entries. Colour tags: Ours, Opposite, Doubtful (no white).
  • Booths & karyakartas: Supervisors are assigned Village Panchayats (was wards); surveyors and booth agents are assigned parts.
  • Data quality: Spelling fixes is removed.
  • Correct roll record: Only the super admin may change official roll data. Others get “Access denied (403)”.

REMOVED

  • Spelling fixes screen.
  • Survey: White colour tag, Important voter and its position, Two-wheeler, Mandal / Group.
  • Family head. The Important voters filter (the Influencer filter takes its place).

Area levels (Goa)

  • Assembly Constituency (AC) → Village Panchayat or Municipal Council → Part / Booth. Goa has two levels of local bodies (Village Panchayat and Zilla Panchayat) and Municipal Councils for towns, so a part belongs either to a village panchayat or to a municipal council (Pernem town). “ZP” means Zilla Panchayat. Every report can be seen at each of the three levels.

Points to confirm, and change requests

Please reply on these with your approval.

Points for you to confirm

PointWhat we need
Kutumb ConnectIn this flow the surveyor makes each Kutumb: types a name (usually the name of the head of the family) and adds or removes members. Nothing is created from house numbers. Your list (item 7) asks for automatic creation from the house number. Please confirm which one you want.
Bulk messageIn this flow the bulk option is WhatsApp to at most 5 voters at a time, through the phone's own WhatsApp. There is no bulk SMS and no separate bulk screen. Please confirm that this covers items 24 and 27.
Village PanchayatsPlease send the list of parts in each Village Panchayat and in Pernem Municipal Council. The names on these screens are only samples.
Previous electionsPlease send the MP, AC and ZP election figures you want shown: which years, and whether they are for the whole constituency, per panchayat or per part.
Influencer listKept exactly as you wrote it. In Goa the post is usually called “Deputy Sarpanch”; “Up Sarpanch” can be renamed under Lists & labels.
Reports on the phoneReports need internet. A surveyor gets them for the assigned parts, a supervisor for the assigned panchayats, an admin for the whole constituency.
